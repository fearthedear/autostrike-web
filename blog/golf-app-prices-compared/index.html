Where the other apps are strong

A fair comparison should say where the competition does well:

  • Free tiers. 18Birdies, Golfshot, Hole19 and SwingU all give you GPS yardages for free [7] [8] [9] [10]. AutoStrike is free to download and free to try for your first few rounds. After that, you need Pro.
  • 18Birdies has a huge following, with 4.9 stars from about 306,000 ratings on the US App Store and free GPS on 45,000+ courses [6].
  • Golfshot has a long history on the wrist. It calls itself "the original Apple Watch golf GPS since 2015" and lists 47,000+ courses [8].
  • Hole19 has the cheapest premium plan of the four, at $69.99 a year, and its Apple Watch distances are free [9].
  • SwingU offers a cheaper Plus tier at $59.99 a year as well as Pro [4].

AutoStrike is a newer app, so it doesn't have that kind of review history yet. What it offers is the core feature set at a much lower price.
