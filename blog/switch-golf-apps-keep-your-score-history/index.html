How to switch golf apps without losing your score history

Key takeaways

  • Most golf apps make it hard to take your data with you. 18Birdies says its playing data can't be exported to a spreadsheet, and we couldn't find a documented export for Golfshot, Hole19 or SwingU.
  • Every app shows your scorecards on screen, though, and screenshots work as a universal export. AutoStrike's AI import reads screenshots and photos of paper scorecards and adds the courses and scores to your history.
  • Switching apps doesn't change your official Handicap Index. That comes from your golf club or association, not from a scoring app.

How to move your scores to AutoStrike, step by step

  1. Screenshot your past rounds. In your old app, open each round's scorecard and take a screenshot. For paper scorecards, take a photo.
  2. Download AutoStrike and open Import Scores.
  3. Select the images from your photo gallery and upload them.
  4. Let the AI do the rest. The courses and scores are added to your round history, and we email you when the import is done.

Tips for a clean import

  • Capture the whole scorecard, including the course name and date if your old app shows them.
  • Make sure the numbers are sharp and not cut off. Zoom out rather than splitting one round across many images.
  • For paper scorecards, shoot flat and in good light.
  • Keep your old app installed until your import email arrives, in case you need another screenshot.

What carries over, and what doesn't

The import works from what is visible in your images, so it's built for courses and scores. Those land in your AutoStrike round history next to the rounds you play with AutoStrike.

Shot-by-shot GPS tracks, club data, photos and social posts from your old app won't come across. If those matter to you, keep a copy, for example by requesting your data from the old app before you cancel.

What happens to your handicap?

An official Handicap Index isn't owned by a scoring app. In the US, the USGA says you get one through a golf club or course, your Allied Golf Association, or usga.org [8], and it describes the Handicap Index as "a portable number" [9]. Switching scoring apps doesn't change it. Keep posting your scores through your club or association.

AutoStrike calculates its own handicap index from your rounds using World Handicap System rules. It's useful for tracking your progress, but it doesn't replace an official Handicap Index.

When to switch

  • Before your renewal date. App Store subscriptions renew automatically. You can cancel in your iPhone's Settings under your name, then Subscriptions [10].
  • After you've captured your history. Take your screenshots first. If your old app has a free tier, your rounds may stay viewable after you cancel, but check before you delete your account.

Frequently asked questions

Can I export my rounds from 18Birdies?

18Birdies says its playing data can not be exported to CSV, Excel or other spreadsheet files. It does have a page where you can request a download of your account data. To move your scores into AutoStrike, screenshots of your 18Birdies scorecards work with AI import.

Can I import scores from another golf app into AutoStrike?

Yes. Take screenshots of past rounds in any golf app, or photos of paper scorecards, and upload them in Import Scores. AI adds the courses and scores to your round history, and we email you when the import is done.

Will I lose my handicap if I switch golf apps?

No. An official Handicap Index is issued through an authorized golf club or association, not a scoring app, and the USGA describes it as portable. Keep posting scores through your club or association. AutoStrike also calculates its own handicap index from your rounds on World Handicap System rules, for tracking.

Does AutoStrike import shot-by-shot data from my old app?

No. The import is built for courses and scores read from your images. Shot-by-shot GPS tracks, club data and photos from your old app do not come across.
